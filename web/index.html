Why Skimmer

Why traders set it and forget it

Everything about the product is built to be easy, honest, and automatic — so the good habit happens without you thinking about it.

Simple by design

Accessible to everyone

It works with the wallet you already have. No new seed phrase to write down, no new account to learn.

Profitable by default

Saving compounds quietly in the background, without needing willpower at the exact moment you are busy trading.

Set and forget

Once configured it just runs. You do not log in, top it up, or remember to move anything — it happens on its own.
